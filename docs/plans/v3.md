# v3 plan: Cart and closing the v2 gaps

Agreed in the design session on 2026-10-26. Vocabulary lives in `GLOSSARY.md`, hard-to-reverse decisions in `docs/adr/` (new: ADR 0005, Cart writes, never checkout). Endpoints come from reading the website's bundles (`f4b80aa.js`, `9478903.js`) and were confirmed by browser and library captures on 2026-10-26.

## Scope

- Cart in the library and the CLI. Home Assistant is unchanged.
- Released as `0.3.0`. The only breaking change is Article Number in search.
- Out: choosing a Pickup Slot or Pickup Point, placing, paying for or cancelling an Order (ADR 0005), a Home Assistant Cart action, batch line changes (`POST ChangeLinesCount`), stock or assortment checks before a change, `--limit` on `pickup-points` (22 Pickup Points; v2 user story 11 is read as `--json` only).

## Article Number

- The Article Number is Bilka's `objectID`, which Order Lines and the Cart use (`productId` in `ChangeLineCount`).
- Breaking: `Product.article_number` from search changes from Algolia's `article` to `objectID`. `article` is dropped.

## Cart

- `get_cart()` returns Cart Lines (Product, Article Number, quantity, Price, line total), the Cart total with the breakdown Bilka gives (Deposit, Service Fee, Packaging, Discount) and the chosen Pickup Point, from `GET shop/v6/Cart?u=w&extra=deliveryAddress,deliveryDate`. Cart Lines are nested like Order Lines (`lines[].lines[].orderlines[]`, `quantity`, `unitprice`, `amount`), amounts in øre.
- Every Cart, even an empty one, has a "Papirpose" line in the group "Emballage" and a "Pakkeservice" line in "Services". They are not Cart Lines; they show up as Packaging and Service Fee in the breakdown.
- A Multibuy Offer is already applied in the line (10 Bananer at 2,50 = 25,00) and counted in `stat.promo`.
- `set_cart_quantity(article_number, quantity)`: `GET shop/v6/ChangeLineCount?u=w&productId=<objectID>&count=<quantity>&fullCart=0`. The count is absolute; 0 removes the Cart Line.
- `add_to_cart(article_number, quantity=1)` reads the Cart and sets the current quantity plus `quantity`. `remove_from_cart(article_number)` sets 0.
- `clear_cart()`: `GET shop/v6/EmptyCart?u=w&extra=deliveryAddress,deliveryDate`. Library only.
- A change answers `{unitCount, sum, productId, productAmount, productUnitPrice, message, offerLimitMessage, eid: -1}`, not the Cart (`productUnitPrice` is in fact the line total), so every change reads the Cart again and returns it.
- `eid` is `-1` on success; a Cart read answered `310` once and `-1` on 2026-10-27, so it is ignored.
- A non-empty `message` or `offerLimitMessage` on an applied change is a warning: returned with the Cart and printed by the CLI, exit 0. Seen: 99 cucumbers applied with "Ring venligst til kundeservice på 89 30 30 00 inden bestilling, for at sikre det ønskede antal"; 100 applied without a message.
- `CartChangeRejectedError` (with Bilka's message) is raised when `productAmount` differs from the requested count.
- The Cart belongs to the customer's account: a Cart filled in the browser is the same Cart the library reads with its own session, and a change from either side shows up in the other.
- A login on the website or in the app can replace the Cart with the one that client saw at its previous login (live check, #26). The library logs in without a `cart` cookie so its own logins never do; details in `docs/agents/capturing.md`, How Bilka's Cart and login behave.

## CLI

- `bilkatogo cart` shows Cart Lines, total and Pickup Point.
- `cart add <article> [--quantity N]`, `cart set <article> <N>`, `cart remove <article>` print the updated Cart and any warning from Bilka. No confirmation prompt, no `cart clear`.
- `--json` on all of them. A rejection prints Bilka's message and exits non-zero.

## Raw captures

- `--raw` on `order` and `cart` prints Bilka's untouched JSON; `--redact` replaces name, email, address, phone, Pickup Code and Order Number with placeholders.
- Captures are redacted before they are committed.

## Captures

Captured on 2026-10-26 in the browser and redacted (`uid`, `tid`):

- `tests/fixtures/cart_logged_in.json`: a logged-in Cart with five Cart Lines, including a Multibuy Offer (10 Bananer). The logged-in `deliveryAddress` has the same fields as the anonymous one.
- `tests/fixtures/change_line_count.json`: an applied change without a message.
- `tests/fixtures/change_line_count_warning.json`: an applied change with Bilka's warning.

No Product had a limit per customer at Store 1661, so no rejection was captured.

## Testing

- The fake Bilka backend gets a Cart that keeps state across `ChangeLineCount`, `EmptyCart` and `Cart`, and can be told to answer a change with a warning or to apply a different quantity than asked.
- Before release the maintainer runs each write once against their own Cart. Never in CI.

## v2 gaps

- Logged-in Cart: closed by `cart_logged_in.json`.
- Missing Item shape and `stat.oos`, Discount sign and vouchers, and an Offer limit rejection: `needs-info` follow-up tickets that replace the synthetic fixtures or assumptions once `--raw --redact` or the browser has captured a real one.

## Unverified assumptions

- That Bilka caps a change at the Offer limit (so `productAmount` is lower than `count`) rather than refusing it some other way.
- What Bilka does when a Product is added with no Pickup Point or Pickup Slot chosen.
- Whether `fullCart=1` would return the Cart and save the second request.
- The maximum quantity per Cart Line.
